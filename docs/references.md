# 参考文献 / References

本流程所用的核心软件与方法学文献。请在论文中补全你实际使用的软件**版本号**。

## 软件 / Software & toolboxes

- **EEGLAB** — Delorme, A., & Makeig, S. (2004). EEGLAB: an open source toolbox
  for analysis of single-trial EEG dynamics including independent component
  analysis. *Journal of Neuroscience Methods, 134*(1), 9–21.
- **ICLabel** — Pion-Tonachini, L., Kreutz-Delgado, K., & Makeig, S. (2019).
  ICLabel: An automated electroencephalographic independent component
  classifier, dataset, and website. *NeuroImage, 198*, 181–197.
- **clean_rawdata / ASR** — Mullen, T. R., Kothe, C. A. E., Chi, Y. M.,
  Ojeda, A., Kerth, T., Makeig, S., Jung, T.-P., & Cauwenberghs, G. (2015).
  Real-time neuroimaging and cognitive monitoring using wearable dry EEG.
  *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, 62*(11), 2553–2567.
- **Microstate EEGlab toolbox** — Poulsen, A. T., Pedroni, A., Langer, N., &
  Hansen, L. K. (2018). Microstate EEGlab toolbox: An introductory guide.
  *bioRxiv*, 289850. <https://github.com/atpoulsen/Microstate-EEGlab-toolbox>

## 微状态方法 / Microstate methodology

- Lehmann, D., Ozaki, H., & Pal, I. (1987). EEG alpha map series: brain
  micro-states by space-oriented adaptive segmentation. *Electroencephalography
  and Clinical Neurophysiology, 67*(3), 271–288.
- Pascual-Marqui, R. D., Michel, C. M., & Lehmann, D. (1995). Segmentation of
  brain electrical activity into microstates: model estimation and validation.
  *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, 42*(7), 658–665.
- Koenig, T., Prichep, L., Lehmann, D., Sosa, P. V., Braeker, E., Kleinlogel,
  H., Isenhart, R., & John, E. R. (2002). Millisecond by millisecond, year by
  year: normative EEG microstates and developmental stages. *NeuroImage, 16*(1),
  41–48.
- Khanna, A., Pascual-Leone, A., Michel, C. M., & Farzan, F. (2015).
  Microstates in resting-state EEG: current status and future directions.
  *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 49*, 105–113.
- Michel, C. M., & Koenig, T. (2018). EEG microstates as a tool for studying the
  temporal dynamics of whole-brain neuronal networks: A review.
  *NeuroImage, 180*, 577–593.
